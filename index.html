How the figures are worked out

  • Daily view shows the selected date. Monthly view covers the 1st of the selected month up to the selected date. Yearly view covers 01-Apr of the financial year up to the selected date.
  • Water, gypsum, limestone, ball mill and dewatering figures are totals over the active period.
  • Load, SOx and opacity are averages of the filled-in days in the active period.
  • APC is shown as stored, without multiplying by 100. Monthly uses the APC_MONTHLY column; Yearly is the average of daily APC from 01-Apr.
  • Availability: Daily = hours ÷ 24. Monthly = the stored monthly %. Yearly = total hours ÷ (days × 24) from 01-Apr. Run hours are totals over the active period.
  • Trends: Daily = selected date ±5 days. Monthly = every day of the selected month. Yearly = monthly averages, plotted on the 1st of each month.
  • DG availability is shown in Daily view only. Total defects and the defect chart come straight from the DEFECT sheet.
